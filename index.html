About

Owen "BeanBaller" Bean is a proudly washed competitive VALORANT player from the NA region, best known for overthinking rounds, calling for teamwork, and insisting on proper utility usage. He primarily plays Astra, leveraging her powerful utility to support a lurking, smokes-heavy playstyle. When the map or team demands it, he reluctantly fills other utility-heavy agents.

BeanBaller competed in the ECAC during the spring semester with the University of Southern Maine (USM) Esports Club, where-despite being washed-his consistency and dedication earned him the Rookie of the Month title. The season concluded at the end of the semester, aligning with his graduation from USM with a degree in Computer Science.
