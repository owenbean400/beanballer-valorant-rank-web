Privacy Policy

The BeanBaller Val Rank Info app and the BeanBaller website collect anonymous analytics and crash logs through Firebase to improve performance and reliability. No personal information is collected within the app.

For users who participate in Valorant ranked games in the same match as BeanBaller, gameplay data is collected and displayed within this app in accordance with Riot Games' Valorant Terms of Service.
